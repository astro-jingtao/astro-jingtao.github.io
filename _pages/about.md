My name is Tao Jing (荆韬), and I am a final-year PhD student in astrophysics at Tsinghua University. My research focuses on stellar feedback and its impact on star formation and quenching processes, as well as the interaction of the multiphase interstellar medium. I also investigate the microstructure of shock- or photon-ionized nebulae to constrain ionization models and improve hydrodynamic simulations. Additionally, I am interested in customizing and adapting advanced machine learning techniques for mining astronomical data. My publications are available on [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/user/libraries/V5BkqDURRE-VkA1cl6fWjg).
